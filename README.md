## ❖ About

I am a **Data & Software Engineer** specializing in industrial analytics, process automation, and intelligent enterprise dashboards. I bridge the gap between complex industrial data systems and user-centric software, transforming raw telemetry into intuitive, real-time control mechanisms. 

My engineering philosophy centers on **product engineering mindset**: I do not just write code; I architect solutions that optimize workflows, reduce human error, and drive operational efficiency. With deep expertise in integrating legacy industrial APIs (Maximo, PI Vision, LabWare) with modern frontend and backend paradigms, I build robust data pipelines and highly interactive UI/UX experiences.
